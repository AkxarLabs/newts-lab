# Critique: the method

How this lab reads a critique ensemble's output by default. The PI can add to it or replace it
(dashboard → Compose). The contract (SKILL.md) fixes the fresh-context invariant, the ensemble, the
score aggregation, the fatal-flaw veto and the oversight checks. The lens definitions themselves live in
`templates/review/critique-lenses.md`.

## Weighing the reviews

- Grade every weakness against the taste rubric in `critique-lenses.md`:
  - **LOAD-BEARING** points drive decisions;
  - **GENERIC** and **MISDIRECTED** points are noted but carry no action items.
- Synthesize: where the lenses agree, where they conflict, and which conflict matters for the decision
  at hand.
- Own drafts: write action items that are specific and checkable ("add the n=3 seed band to Table 2"),
  never general ("improve clarity").

## External papers: what to take away

- The distilled note says what the critique means for *our* positioning. For example: "claimed SOTA is
  unsupported at our scale; the baseline is weaker than it appears".
- Report the one finding that most changes a decision, not a summary of every lens.
